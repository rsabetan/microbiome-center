Ways to work with us

Support that meets your study where it is

Engage at whatever level fits — from early design guidance to a full end-to-end partnership. Each level builds on the one before it.

Kickstart

Early guidance to get the design right before you collect anything — study and sampling design, IRB and consent language, and collection protocols.

Build

Hands-on setup of the operational workflow — pre-assembled kits, coordinator support, REDCap and data capture, and biospecimen storage.

Comprehensive

An end-to-end partnership through analysis — sequencing, microbiome analysis, clinical–microbiome data linkage, and interpretation.
